# Prospective span-robustness method

`evaluate_sliding_windows` runs one frozen, allowlisted, non-locus method fixture. It binds the
fixture sequence bytes and SHA-256 plus explicit accessible-element and silencer geometry, then
slides a fixed-width overlapping or contiguous ASO footprint and records the approved engine's predicted inclusion direction. It summarizes the modal
direction among non-abstaining windows and direction flips between adjacent, non-abstaining
windows. Abstentions are reported and never counted as agreement or flips.

This is in-silico method tooling only. It does not consume outcomes, does not estimate a biological
span effect, and does not activate architecture pillar P4's data-conditional benchmark. STMN2 and
UNC13A are retained only as unevaluated leads pending committed cryptic-exon contexts and qualifying
matched intervention reads.

These metrics are valid only for that explicit fixture geometry. They are an engine-wiring check,
not locus inference. Arbitrary identifiers, accessions, sequences, and callbacks are not accepted.
Reports also bind the exact perturbation specification and frozen engine identity/config. Their
render-time validator deterministically reruns the approved predictor from the bound sequence;
callers cannot inject a predictor or serialize invented directions. The shipped boundary executes
the real `EffectorPhysicsAdapter` with `DeterministicStubFolder`, a typed ASO complex, and the frozen
fixture event. Provenance includes full `ModelConfig`, its protocol hash, backend version, and
adapter/backend/package source hashes. It does not consume outcomes.
