### Is there a Goldilocks point?
  best margin: +7.15 kcal/mol at length 18
  margin monotonic in length: False
  Best margin is at an ENDPOINT (length 18), so the scanned
  range does not contain an interior optimum. The true optimum, if
  any, lies outside 6-18 and this scan cannot locate it.

### Caveats that travel with these numbers
  - The off-target panel is 4 validated hits + 20 length-matched
    controls, not the transcriptome. Axis 1 is transcriptome-wide;
    axis 2 is not. A panel margin is not a transcriptome guarantee.
  - Length 18 is nusinersen's published site, NOT a scan winner: no
    18-mer has a span-robust footprint at all (see goldilocks). It is
    a reference point, and a length-18 row is not evidence about what
    an optimised 18-mer could do.
  - Every feasible ASO from 6 to 14 nt lands on the GC core at
    32059-32062, which is the site A3 recovered blind. The length axis
    and the position axis are therefore NOT independent here.
