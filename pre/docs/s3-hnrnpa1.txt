### SCOPE LIMIT OF THE GATE ITSELF -- read before believing the above
  hnRNP A1's high-affinity site is a UAG motif, and BOTH gate RNAs are
  UAG-rich (6DCL AGUAGAUUAGC, 8X0N UUAGGGUUAGGG -- two UAGs each).
  The SMN2 window contains 0 UAG motifs; ISS-N1 (CCAGCAUUAUGAAAG) contains NO UAG.
  So the gate established competence precisely where the motif is
  present, and this target lies OUTSIDE that regime. A gate that only
  proves competence on UAG substrates does not license a claim about a
  UAG-free one. S3's central question is therefore UNRESOLVED, not
  answered in the negative: the absence of predicted UP1 contact on
  ISS-N1 is as consistent with the model being out of its depth as
  with hnRNP A1 not binding there. It is NOT evidence against the
  published hnRNP A1 / ISS-N1 interaction.

  INTERPRETATION LIMIT: the ternary system is a co-occupancy probe.
  Overlap between the UP1 interface and the ISS-N1 nucleotides that
  nusinersen occupies is a STERIC incompatibility argument -- the two
  cannot both be bound. It is not a competition assay and does not
  say which one wins.
