## POST-HOC INTERPRETATION -- declared as post-hoc, changes nothing above
  V1's background base rate at |dPSI| >= 0.2 is 55.8% here, versus 11.5% on GSE307431.
  CAUSE: Ottesen's supplementary tables are already filtered to
  events the authors called notable, so small-effect events are
  absent and the background is itself enriched for large effects.
  CONSEQUENCE: 'enrichment above background' has little room to
  register when background is already the majority. V1 was a
  WEAKER test than intended, and its null is correspondingly weaker
  evidence than a null on a full measured background would be.
  V2 IS NOT AFFECTED -- the dose-retention correlation never touches
  background composition. It is the cleaner negative of the two, and
  it is the one with a real n (123).
  This does NOT rescue V1: the observed fold is 0.90, BELOW 1.
